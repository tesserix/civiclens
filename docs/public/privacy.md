CIVICLENS · TESSERIX

# Privacy policy

Effective 22 October 2026. CivicLens is operated by Tesserix Pty Ltd, ACN 694 070 865, 5 Tagu Place, Kings Park NSW 2148, Australia. CivicLens is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

## What we collect and why

Apple, Google or Meta sign-in is handled by our Tesserix identity service. That service may receive your provider identifier, name and email for authentication. CivicLens privately links the verified identifier to an account and public nickname. We store revocable sessions, report photos, descriptions, categories, chosen sharing destinations, consent, precise coordinates and locality to process reports and determine responsible offices. Support correspondence and security records help us respond to requests and protect the service.

## Device access

Camera and foreground location are requested when you choose to use them. Choosing an existing image uses the system photo picker. We do not need continuous background location, your microphone, contacts, health data or access to your entire photo library. Drafts and report photos can remain on your device until removed. Signing out does not itself erase local drafts.

## Public sharing and processors

Saved reports are private in CivicLens. With your confirmation, sanitized photos, text, nickname and approximate location can be handed to your selected social apps immediately without moderator approval. You finish the post using your own account. Managed publishing on an enabled CivicLens account and official complaint preparation require separate review and consent. Exact stored coordinates and your sign-in name/email are not included in public captions. Images and text can still identify people: a nickname is not a guarantee of anonymity. Do not submit private people, sensitive locations or unsupported allegations.

We use Google Cloud hosting, the Tesserix identity service, Cloudflare network protection, and your chosen sign-in provider. Enabled social publishing uses Meta services. If you use writing help, your photo, category and text are sent to Google Cloud Vertex AI through the Tesserix AI gateway to suggest a description; suggestions apply only when you choose them, and CivicLens does not store the request. We also send writing-help notes, generated suggestions and the final public caption through this gateway for language-safety screening. Rejected text is not echoed in errors or included in application logs; final captions are held when safety cannot be verified. Provider permissions and availability vary; choosing a destination is not a guarantee of publication. Data is hosted in India and may be processed in Australia, the United States and other provider locations. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising tracking in this release.

## Optional account profile

You may save a private profile photo and a short bio. These are visible only in your own account and are not added to public reports, community cards or social captions. If your verified sign-in provider supplies a supported photo, you can explicitly import it, preview it and choose whether to save it. We keep only the private photo candidate URL and sanitized photo, not provider access tokens, names or email addresses. Remove the photo or edit the bio in Account. These fields are included in account export and deletion.

## Sharing through your own accounts

If you choose user sharing, CivicLens prepares the caption and metadata-stripped photos you have confirmed for a native handoff or your phone’s share sheet. No moderator approval is needed. The caption passes automatic language-safety screening; you remain responsible for checking the photos and final post. You choose an installed app and destination and confirm there. That app may display your profile name, username, Page name or phone number even when the caption credits your CivicLens nickname. No additional social-provider login or publishing token is collected for this native handoff. You manage group membership and community rules directly with the destination service. For TikTok, you can also explicitly save the confirmed photos to your photo library, copy the public caption to the clipboard and open TikTok to upload them yourself. Saved photos may sync through your device’s photo backup settings. Copies remain until you remove them; an interrupted save may already have created a copy. CivicLens cannot verify that a share-sheet action was posted or remove copies controlled by your account, members or the destination platform.

## Community requests and opinion polls

When you use these features, we store your requests, moderated opinions, consent, private responses and abuse reports. Approved requests and opinions show your nickname. Individual poll and request choices stay private; only aggregate counts are shown, with poll results held until closing. These choices can reveal political or civic views. Participation is optional. Account identity does not verify electoral eligibility or one unique human. Private choices are not provided to other participants or advertisers.

## Official complaint preparation

When requested, we store your reviewed factual complaint text, selected official destination, consent and preparation/handoff status. Preparing or opening a form does not submit a government complaint. You provide any required contact details directly to that service and follow its privacy terms. CivicLens does not receive an official filing receipt from a native handoff. Saved evidence and copies you send through other apps remain under your and those services’ control.

## Internal official complaint requests

If you choose this feature, we store a private link to your report, your selected administrative region and district, provider-readiness metadata, consent audit references and creation time. Current requests are blocked internal records, not filed police complaints. This gateway does not send photos, collect legal identity or create an official government reference. These records are included in account export and deletion handling.

## Retention and deletion

Account data, reports, community content, responses and complaint preparation records are retained while needed to operate your account, until you request deletion, subject to a documented legal obligation. Initiate deletion in Account → Delete my account or use the external [account deletion page](account-deletion.md). Tesserix processes requests within 30 days and confirms completion and any specific retention exception. A pending receipt is not completed erasure.

We remove associated data from active systems and coordinate identity/provider cleanup. Recovery backups expire under the applicable backup retention policy; the completion confirmation states the verified expiry and any legal exception. Restored data must be subject to the same deletion request. We cannot guarantee removal of screenshots, reposts or copies controlled by others. Uninstall or clear CivicLens data on each device to remove local drafts and photos.

## Your rights and contact

You may request access, correction, export or deletion, or raise a privacy concern with [sales@tesserix.app](mailto:sales@tesserix.app). You may also complain to your applicable privacy regulator. We explain any legally required restriction rather than claiming universal anonymity or immediate deletion. The [Tesserix privacy policy](https://tesserix.app/privacy) provides company-wide details; this page explains CivicLens-specific processing.
