# CivicLens child-safety release checklist

Status: the owner approved the policy rollout and confirmed contact readiness
on 25 October 2026. Production verification is still required. Do not submit
Google's compliance attestation until the published page and reporting path
have been verified.

## Published standards and contact

The existing public terms route will serve the standards at
`https://civiclns-api.tesserix.app/v1/terms#child-safety` after an approved API
rollout. No mobile change, new endpoint, credential or migration is needed.
The proposed contact uses the existing CivicLens support address,
`sales@tesserix.app`; the owner confirmed that the contact is ready to handle
child-safety concerns and required authority reports before the rollout.

Google requires a globally accessible HTML policy against CSAE, an in-app
reporting mechanism, a capable designated contact, and a declaration covering
applicable law and reports to regional/national authorities. An adults-only
audience does not exempt this social-category app.

## Response procedure

1. Assign a trained owner and backup to the contact mailbox and moderation
   queue. Confirm access, escalation ownership and regional reporting duties.
2. Receive community reports through the existing in-app reporting control or
   the support link in the terms available from the sign-in screen. Never ask
   the reporter to attach suspected CSAM or redistribute it for investigation.
3. Triage child-safety concerns promptly. Restrict case access to the designated
   handlers, record internal references, and withhold/remove prohibited content.
   Use the existing moderation controls; do not publish it to social apps.
4. Restrict abusive accounts through the established identity/operator process.
   Preserve only legally required evidence and audit information under restricted
   access and the applicable retention rules; never put evidence in Git, email,
   chat, public logs or test fixtures.
5. Notify the appropriate regional/national authority as legally required,
   including NCMEC when applicable. The assigned handler verifies the lawful
   channel and reporting obligations; this is a manual escalation, not an
   implemented automated law-enforcement API.
6. Record the action and authority acknowledgement privately, cooperate with
   lawful requests, and apply retention/deletion rules. For immediate danger,
   direct the reporter to local emergency services.

## Release evidence required

- Confirm the mailbox owner and backup, and their readiness to discuss
  prevention practices with Google. Do not claim monitoring or reporting that
  has not been set up.
- Exercise in-app reporting using an ordinary synthetic safety concern, never
  illegal material. Verify that authorized moderators can see the report and
  can restrict the relevant content.
- Approve and roll out the public terms change, then verify the production URL
  without authentication from outside the cluster.
- Obtain the owner's action-time approval for Google's two child-safety
  declarations and save the form with the verified URL and contact.

This document and a checked Play Console box do not themselves establish legal
compliance. No real child-safety incident has been reported or simulated here.
