Delete your CivicLens account

CivicLens is operated by Tesserix Pty Ltd. You can request deletion without installing the app.

From the app

Open the Account tab → Delete my account → Request account deletion. Confirm the request and keep its receipt. Our team processes it within 30 days. You do not need to send an additional email to initiate a request made in the app.

Without the app

Email sales@tesserix.app with the subject “CivicLens account deletion”. Include your public nickname and the sign-in provider/email you used so we can verify ownership. Never send a password, verification code or access token. We may verify ownership before deleting the account; no explanation for leaving is required.

What deletion covers

We remove your CivicLens profile, account identifiers, sessions, report photos, precise locations, descriptions and associated publication data from active systems, coordinate identity/provider cleanup, and confirm completion within 30 days. Your other Tesserix accounts and the underlying Apple, Google or Meta account are preserved.

Copies held by other people or platforms may remain outside our control. Any legally necessary exception and verified backup expiry are explained in the completion confirmation. Clearing app data or uninstalling on each device removes local drafts and photos; exported copies require separate removal. A request receipt indicates pending work, not completed deletion.

See the privacy policy for collection and retention details.
