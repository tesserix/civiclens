Terms and community rules

CivicLens is provided by Tesserix Pty Ltd for adults aged 18 and over. The Tesserix Terms of Service apply. Contact sales@tesserix.app for support, complaints or takedown requests.

Constructive reporting

Only submit content you have the right to share. Report observable civic issues accurately and safely. Do not trespass, put yourself or others in danger, identify private individuals unnecessarily, publish private addresses or sensitive locations, or submit threats, hate, harassment, sexual content, violence, illegal material, impersonation, spam or unsupported allegations. Never submit content exploiting or endangering children.

Review and responsibility

Sharing a report through your own social account does not require moderator approval. You must check every photo and the caption, take responsibility for your post and confirm it in the destination app. Automatic language-safety checks and photo metadata stripping still apply. Community requests and opinions, managed publishing and official complaint preparation have separate moderation and verification requirements. We may withhold or remove content and restrict abusive accounts. A report is an allegation or observation, not a finding of guilt. CivicLens is not a government service, police reporting channel or emergency dispatch system. In immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Child safety standards

CivicLens prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexual solicitation of minors, sextortion, trafficking and any content or conduct that facilitates the sexual exploitation of children. These rules apply to photos, text, community requests, opinions, profiles and sharing. CivicLens is for adults aged 18 and over; the age limit does not reduce these protections.

Use the report control on community content to raise a child-safety concern. You can also open these rules from the app and contact sales@tesserix.app with the subject “CivicLens child safety”. Include the content or report reference and a brief description. Do not email, download, copy or forward suspected CSAM. If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first.

Our child-safety contact receives concerns and coordinates a restricted review. We withhold or remove prohibited content and restrict offending accounts. Necessary evidence and audit information are handled with limited access and applicable retention requirements, rather than redistributed through social sharing. We report suspected CSAM and exploitation to the appropriate regional or national authority as required by applicable law, including NCMEC when required. We cooperate with lawful investigations.

Your content

You retain ownership of your content. By requesting sharing, you authorize CivicLens to process your photos and caption and prepare them for your selected destinations. For user sharing, you choose and confirm the post in your social app using your own account. No moderator approval is required for this handoff. You control communities you own, invites and membership there, and must follow that service’s rules. Your external sender identity may be visible. CivicLens does not verify delivery from a share-sheet action. Sharing is subject to safety checks, provider support and permissions. Managed publishing and official complaint preparation retain independent review. Do not expect every requested channel or every country's authority directory to be available. Public content may be copied by others.

Requests and opinion polls

Use fair questions and respectful opinions. New requests and opinions need independent review. One current response per authenticated account can be changed or withdrawn until the server deadline. Account-backed support is not a legal digital signature, government petition or verified election vote. Polls are voluntary, may be unrepresentative, and have no money, wagering, market price or prize. Poll results appear after closing; rounded percentages include Yes, No and Don’t know. Support and opinion records are subject to moderation and the privacy policy.

Government complaint handoff

Official channels require separate verification. Confirm the responsible body, factual text and reviewed photos before preparing evidence. You complete and submit any official form and supply required identity yourself. CivicLens preparation and handoff records are not government acknowledgements or guarantees of action. No automated filing is available without a documented and authorized provider integration. Never use this app to request emergency dispatch.

Privacy and deletion

Read the privacy policy and account deletion instructions. Signing out is different from deleting your account. This internal preview is not an emergency or guaranteed response service.
