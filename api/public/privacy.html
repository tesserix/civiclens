Privacy policy

Effective 22 October 2026. CivicLens is operated by Tesserix Pty Ltd, ACN 694 070 865, 5 Tagu Place, Kings Park NSW 2148, Australia. CivicLens is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

What we collect and why

Apple, Google or Meta sign-in is handled by our Tesserix identity service. That service may receive your provider identifier, name and email for authentication. CivicLens privately links the verified identifier to an account and public nickname. We store revocable sessions, report photos, descriptions, categories, chosen sharing destinations, consent, precise coordinates and locality to process reports and determine responsible offices. Support correspondence and security records help us respond to requests and protect the service.

Device access

Camera and foreground location are requested when you choose to use them. Choosing an existing image uses the system photo picker. We do not need continuous background location, your microphone, contacts, health data or access to your entire photo library. Drafts and report photos can remain on your device until removed. Signing out does not itself erase local drafts.

Public sharing and processors

Reports remain private until reviewed. With your consent, reviewed photos, text, nickname and approximate location may be published on an enabled CivicLens social account and sent to verified public authorities. Exact stored coordinates and your sign-in name/email are not included in public captions. Images and text can still identify people: a nickname is not a guarantee of anonymity. Do not submit private people, sensitive locations or unsupported allegations.

We use Google Cloud hosting, the Tesserix identity service, Cloudflare network protection, and your chosen sign-in provider. Enabled social publishing uses Meta services. If you use writing help, your photo, category and text are sent to Google Cloud Vertex AI through the Tesserix AI gateway to suggest a description; suggestions apply only when you choose them, and CivicLens does not store the request. We also send writing-help notes, generated suggestions and the final reviewed public caption through this gateway for language-safety screening. Rejected text is not echoed in errors or included in application logs; final captions are held when safety cannot be verified. Provider permissions and availability vary; choosing a destination is not a guarantee of publication. Data is hosted in India and may be processed in Australia, the United States and other provider locations. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising tracking in this release.

Retention and deletion

Account data and reports are retained while needed to operate your account, until you request deletion, subject to a documented legal obligation. Initiate deletion in Account → Delete my account or use the external account deletion page. Tesserix processes requests within 30 days and confirms completion and any specific retention exception. A pending receipt is not completed erasure.

We remove associated data from active systems and coordinate identity/provider cleanup. Recovery backups expire under the applicable backup retention policy; the completion confirmation states the verified expiry and any legal exception. Restored data must be subject to the same deletion request. We cannot guarantee removal of screenshots, reposts or copies controlled by others. Uninstall or clear CivicLens data on each device to remove local drafts and photos.

Your rights and contact

You may request access, correction, export or deletion, or raise a privacy concern with sales@tesserix.app. You may also complain to your applicable privacy regulator. We explain any legally required restriction rather than claiming universal anonymity or immediate deletion. The Tesserix privacy policy provides company-wide details; this page explains CivicLens-specific processing.
